Professional summary

I am a materials scientist and engineer specializing in complex fluids and soft matter. I combine theory, computational physics and experiments to develop physical insight and help design advanced materials. I value collaborative, interdisciplinary and international science.

Research experience

Columbia University · Postdoctoral Research Scientist2023 — Present

I collaborate with polymer scientists and engineers across institutions in the US, Europe and India. I have co-supervised, trained and mentored 8 PhD and 10+ BSc/MSc students, contributed to 2 grant proposals (1 internal, 1 NSF) and led 5 research projects.

  • DOE Genesis Mission: “AI-Enabled Physical Operating System for Bio-programmable Matter” (2026–2027), PI Prof. Kyle Bishop.
  • NSF #2225290: “Multiscale Data-Physics Models for the Critical Role of Interfaces in Overmolded Thermoplastic Parts” (2023–2026), PI Prof. Sanat Kumar.

I work on multiphysics simulation of plastics processing, DNA self-assembly and gel thermodynamics; polymer degradation and nanoplastics release; statistical mechanics of polymers and complex fluids in and out of equilibrium; and closed-loop AI-driven experimentation.

Education

Imperial College London · PhD in Theory and Simulation of Materials2019 — 2023

Procter & Gamble fully funded my PhD. I was affiliated with the Thomas Young Centre and the Centre for Process Systems Engineering, and worked with Prof. George Jackson and Prof. Amparo Galindo. My thesis was “Molecular modelling and statistical thermodynamics of semi-crystalline polymers: network constraints, fluid sorption and surface melting”. I explored applications in packaging, drug excipients and reactor-mixture design, and wrote an EPSRC grant proposal on optimizing polymer liners for H₂ storage.

Imperial College London · MSc in Theory and Simulation of Materials2018 — 2019

I graduated with distinction. My thesis was “Modelling the thermodynamic properties of aqueous PEG mixtures”; I focused on computational physics (MD, MC, free-energy methods, DFT, AI/ML) and grant writing.

Stanford University · Undergraduate Summer Visitor, Organic Chemistry IntensiveSummer 2017

I earned an A+ (top 1%) and gained experience with polymer synthesis and analytical techniques.

Politecnico di Milano · BSc in Materials and Nanotechnology Engineering2015 — 2018

I graduated 110/110 cum laude and as valedictorian. My thesis was “Anodic intercalation of graphite: application to the production of graphene and graphene oxide-based systems”.

Teaching

Columbia University · Lecturer, Polymer Physics2024 — 2025

I co-designed and delivered the graduate Polymer Physics course in the Department of Chemical Engineering. I prepared original lecture materials and examined about 20 students.

Imperial College London · Graduate Teaching Assistant2018 — 2023

I completed 400+ hours of marking, tutoring and mentoring in Materials and Chemical Engineering courses, and received the Outstanding GTA Student Choice Award in 2021.

I have been an Associate Fellow of Advance HE since 2023.
