Research

From molecular mechanisms to material performance

I investigate how polymer architecture, thermodynamic interactions and processing history determine the structure, mechanics and degradation of soft and polymeric materials.

01

Semicrystalline polymers: topology, morphology and interfaces

Semicrystalline polymers combine ordered crystallites with amorphous chain segments. I study how chain connectivity, tie molecules and chain ends shape the amorphous-phase topology, and how melting, interdiffusion and recrystallization histories affect interfacial structure. In recent work, I examined melt-memory effects during thermal welding and persistent structural inhomogeneities near weld planes.

Chain topologyCrystallizationThermal weldingMolecular dynamics
02

Polymer degradation and micro-/nanoplastics

I investigate the role of polymer microstructure—particularly amorphous chains and the organization of semicrystalline domains—in micro- and nanoplastics formation and release. I connect degradation mechanisms and photooxidation to changes in microstructure and mechanics, and work toward predictive descriptions of polymer lifetime and material performance.

NanoplasticsPhotooxidationAmorphous domainsPolymer aging
03

Thermodynamics and fluid sorption in polymers

In my doctoral research, I developed molecular thermodynamic descriptions of fluid sorption in semicrystalline and amorphous polymers. I modeled water–polyethylene glycol mixtures with SAFT-γ Mie, studied hydrocarbon sorption in polyethylene and CO₂ solubility in polystyrene and PMMA, and used statistical mechanics to describe semicrystalline polymer morphology and non-equilibrium glassy-polymer behavior.

SAFT-γ MieFluid sorptionEquations of stateStatistical thermodynamics
04

Processing, mechanics and multiphysics interfaces

I develop and apply modeling frameworks for thermoplastic processing that couple thermal transport, crystallization, deformation and nanoscale interdiffusion. I focus on how interfaces form and perform in overmolded thermoplastic parts, and study thermomechanically coupled visco–hyperelastic–plastic behavior and self-heating.

OvermoldingChemo-thermo-mechanicsInterfacial integrityConstitutive modeling
05

Polymer networks, gels and programmable matter

I study the thermodynamics and phase behavior of polymer networks, including reversible crosslinking and networks under tension. I also investigate gel thermodynamics and use multiphysics simulations to study DNA self-assembly, connecting soft-matter physics to bio-programmable materials.

Polymer networksGel thermodynamicsReversible crosslinksDNA self-assembly
06

Methods: theory, computation and experiment

I combine physical modeling, computation and experimental characterization. My work draws on statistical mechanics, polymer physics and condensed matter; molecular dynamics, Monte Carlo and density functional theory; scientific programming and HPC; and SAXS/WAXS, calorimetry, thermogravimetric analysis, GC–MS, FTIR and Raman spectroscopy. I am also exploring closed-loop AI-driven experimentation.
