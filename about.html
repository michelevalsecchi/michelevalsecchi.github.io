About

Multiscale theory and simulation of polymers and soft matter

I combine theory, computational physics and experiments to develop physical insight into complex fluids and soft matter, and to help design advanced materials.

Research perspective

My research is motivated by a recurring question: how do molecular-scale interactions and constraints translate into the structure and behavior of real materials? I approach this question through statistical mechanics, polymer physics and computational modeling, with applications ranging from fluid sorption and semicrystalline polymer morphology to degradation, processing and soft-matter phase behavior.

At Columbia University, I work with polymer scientists and engineers across institutions in the United States, Europe and India. My current research spans the molecular mechanisms of plastic degradation and crystallization, multiphysics simulation of polymer processing, DNA self-assembly and gel thermodynamics. I have led five research projects and co-supervised, trained and mentored eight PhD students and more than ten BSc/MSc students.

Research training

My doctoral work focused on molecular modeling and statistical thermodynamics of semicrystalline polymers, including network constraints, fluid sorption and surface melting. I connected molecular models to applications in packaging, drug excipients and reactor-mixture design, and developed an EPSRC grant proposal on polymer liners for hydrogen storage.

Tools and approaches

Methods I work with

I use a broad toolkit to connect molecular mechanisms, mesoscale structure and measurable material response.

THEORY

Statistical mechanics

I work with polymer physics, condensed matter, self-consistent field theory and thermodynamics.

SIMULATION

Computational physics

I use molecular dynamics (LAMMPS), Monte Carlo and density functional theory (PySCF).

COMPUTING

Scientific software

I work with Python, Julia, Unix/Bash, HPC/SLURM and Git.

CHARACTERIZATION

Experimental methods

I use SAXS/WAXS, DSC/TGA, GC–MS, FTIR and Raman spectroscopy.

RESEARCH PRACTICE

Collaboration & mentoring

EMERGING DIRECTION
