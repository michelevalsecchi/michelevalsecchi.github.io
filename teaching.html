Teaching and supervision

I have taught graduate-level polymer physics and undergraduate/master's courses in materials and chemical engineering. I have prepared original teaching materials, supported students through tutorials and mentoring, and contributed to assessment through marking and examinations.

I draw on my research to teach core ideas in polymer physics, statistical mechanics, thermodynamics and computational materials science, emphasizing the links between molecular structure, material properties and engineering applications.
