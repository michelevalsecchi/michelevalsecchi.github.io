Research directions

Connecting micro and macroscale

I combine statistical mechanics, molecular simulation and multiphysics modelling to understand the mechanisms that govern polymer behaviour, with a particular focus on thermodynamic, mechanical and degradation properties.

01 / STRUCTURE↗

Semicrystalline polymers & interfaces

I study chain topology, amorphous-phase structure, surface melting and the molecular origins of weld behaviour.

 02 / DEGRADATION↗

Polymer degradation & nanoplastics

I investigate how microstructure and amorphous chains contribute to micro- and nanoplastics formation and release.

 03 / THERMODYNAMICS↗

Fluid sorption & polymer thermodynamics

I develop molecular equations of state and statistical-mechanical descriptions of fluids in polymer materials.

 04 / PROCESSING↗

Processing, mechanics & multiphysics

I model coupled thermal, chemical and mechanical processes that shape interfaces in thermoplastic parts.

 05 / SOFT MATTER↗

Networks, gels & self-assembly

I study thermodynamics and phase behaviour in polymer networks, reversible crosslinks and programmable matter.

 06 / METHODS↗

Theory, computation & experiment

I combine statistical mechanics, molecular simulation, continuum modelling and experimental characterization.
